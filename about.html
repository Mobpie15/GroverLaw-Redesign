Institutional History

Three Decades of Strategic Legal Advocacy

Founded in 1996 in New Delhi, Groverlaw was established with a singular institutional premise: to deliver top-tier, partner-led legal counsel combining deep domestic courtroom rigor with international standards of practice.

Vikram Grover

Founding Partner & Lead Counsel

Delhi Chamber & Gurgaon Office

Vikram Grover has been actively practicing before the Supreme Court of India, the High Courts, and the Intellectual Property Office since 1996. In 2002, he was admitted on the Roll of Solicitors of the Supreme Court of England & Wales, establishing a rare dual qualification that enables effortless navigation of cross-border common-law disputes and statutory frameworks.

His practice specializes in IP enforcement, high-value commercial arbitration, and regulatory structuring for foreign multinational entrants into the Indian market.
