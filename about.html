Groverlaw is a law practice led by Vikram Grover. Vikram is a dual-qualified lawyer, enrolled as an Advocate in 1996 in India and admitted on the Roll of Solicitors (England and Wales) in 2002.

We provide consultancy, advisory, and litigation services in matters relating to Intellectual Property and Corporate laws. Our practice is a member of INTA (International Trademark Association) and attorneys of our Firm are active members of APAA (Asian Patent Attorneys Association), ECTA (European Communities Trade Mark Association), IPAA, INBA, and renowned Bar Associations.

Our practice recognizes today's challenging global economic environment and offers legal services with a personalized touch. Strong emphasis is placed on timely and effective delivery. Value-driven excellence and best possible results are attributes which are striven for.

What We Do

Groverlaw's practice provides consultancy, advisory, and litigation services with a strong emphasis on timely, effective delivery and value-driven excellence. The practice is in a position to leverage its existing relationship with several professionals and law firms in noteworthy jurisdictions of the world, offering seamless international representation.

Proprietary Resources

Use of smart technology and vast legal resources assist the practice in meeting clients' objectives. We have comprehensive searchable databases comprising all published trademarks and notifications featured in the Trade Marks Journal, patent entries, and designs published in the Patents Journal. These databases confer an added advantage and facilitate servicing clients' needs with all expedition.

Our Team

We are a team of experienced Attorneys having advised and represented myriad clients across technology, manufacturing, FMCG, and creative sectors. Our team has rich experience in dispute resolution and has represented clients before the Intellectual Property Offices, the Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB / High Court IPD), and various Courts and Tribunals.

Our Services Scope

Groverlaw is a full-service Intellectual Property (IP) boutique law firm which also provides advisory and litigation services in matters relating to Commercial and Corporate laws. We provide an array of services in the IP arena ranging from securing protection and portfolio management to devising enforcement strategies including civil, criminal, and administrative remedies.
