Intellectual Property (IP) Law

From registration and prosecution before IP Offices to enforcement in High Courts and border control seizures.

Patents

Patents Act, 1970

Filing, prosecution, and litigation of Indian and international applications under the PCT. Drafting complete and provisional specifications, priority claims, Form-26 Powers of Attorney within 3 months, expedited examination for Startups, Small entities, and female applicants, Form 27 annual working statements, pre-grant/post-grant oppositions, and compulsory licensing.

  • • Revised Computer Related Inventions (CRIs) Guidelines under Section 3(k)
  • • Section 8 foreign application information & compliance
  • • 20-year term management & divisional application prosecution

Trade Marks

Trade Marks Act, 1999

Searches across proprietary databases of published marks in the Trade Marks Journal. Filing applications under Form TM-A, handling Madrid System international registrations across 100+ countries, non-traditional marks (sound, color, shape), well-known trademark declarations, oppositions, rectifications, and criminal anti-counterfeiting actions.

  • • 10% fee reduction for e-filing; concessional rates for Startups
  • • Counter-statements & cancellation proceedings
  • • Comparative advertising review & honest practices compliance

Industrial Designs

Designs Act, 2000

Protection of features of shape, configuration, pattern, or ornament applied to articles under the Locarno Classification. Processed and prosecuted exclusively through the Design Wing of the Patent Office in Kolkata. Term of 10 years extendable by 5 years.

  • • Mandatory Form 24 for Startup / Small entity fee status
  • • Cancellation petitions before the Controller
  • • Actions for piracy of registered designs

Copyright

Copyright Act, 1957

Protecting original literary, dramatic, musical, artistic works, cinematograph films, sound recordings, and computer software code. Registration with the Copyright Office, author moral rights protection, compulsory and statutory licensing, and copyright societies advisory.

  • • Term of lifetime + 60 years for author works
  • • Clearances from Trade Marks Registry for artistic marks
  • • Criminal complaint remedies before Police & Magistrates

Enforcement & Anti-Counterfeiting

Litigation

  • • Criminal raids with police under Trade Marks & Copyright Acts
  • • Damages and rendition of accounts of profits
