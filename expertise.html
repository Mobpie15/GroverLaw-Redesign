Practice Spectrum

Comprehensive Statutory & Litigation Expertise

Strategic representation and transactional stewardship across every tier of Intellectual Property, Foreign Direct Investment, and Commercial Dispute Resolution in India.

Tier I Core Practice

Intellectual Property (IP) Law

From clearance searches and prosecution before statutory registries to aggressive High Court enforcement and Customs border seizures.

Patents & Inventions

Patents Act, 1970

Drafting provisional and complete specifications, PCT applications and National Phase entry in India, expedited examination for startups and female innovators, Section 3(k) Computer Related Inventions (CRI) guidelines, Form 27 annual working statements, pre-grant/post-grant oppositions, and compulsory licensing.

  • Section 8 foreign patent information compliance
  • Response to First Examination Reports (FER) & hearings
  • Divisional application drafting & 20-year term maintenance

Trade Marks & Brand Names

Trade Marks Act, 1999

Proprietary in-house clearance searches indexing the Trade Marks Journal. Filings under Form TM-A, Madrid Protocol international registrations across 100+ member jurisdictions, non-traditional marks (color, sound, shape), well-known mark determinations, oppositions, and rectifications.

  • Opposition filings & evidence under Rules 45, 46 & 47
  • Passing-off and comparative advertising advisory
  • 10-year renewal management & registered user agreements

Industrial Designs

Designs Act, 2000

Registration of novel shapes, configurations, patterns, and ornaments applied to industrial articles under the Locarno Classification. Applications prosecuted through the specialized Design Wing of the Patent Office in Kolkata. Term of 10 years extendable by 5 years.

  • Cancellation petitions before the Controller
  • Civil actions for piracy of registered industrial designs

Copyright & Software Code

Copyright Act, 1957

Securing statutory copyright protection for proprietary computer software source code, literary and artistic packaging, cinematograph works, and sound recordings. Authors' moral rights protection and copyright society licensing.

  • Search certificate clearance (CCPS) for artistic packaging marks
  • Criminal remedies before Judicial Magistrates and Police
Tier II Tactical Enforcement

Anti-Counterfeiting, Raids & Border Control

Zero-tolerance protection against counterfeit syndicates, unauthorized parallel imports, and market knock-offs.

Courtroom Search Orders (Anton Piller)

Securing ex-parte interim injunctions and appointment of Local Court Commissioners empowered to enter premises, seize infringing stock, inspect ledgers, and seal contraband inventories.

  • Simultaneous multi-city search and seizure executions
  • Criminal FIR registrations & police coordination

Customs IPR Border Recordal

Recordal of registered trademarks, designs, and copyrights with Indian Customs under the Intellectual Property Rights (Imported Goods) Enforcement Rules, 2007 (amended 2018) for automatic port interception.

  • Interception of illicit consignments at major air & sea ports
  • Parallel import and exhaustion of rights analysis
Tier III Corporate Structuring

Corporate, FDI & Commercial Transactions

Guiding international entrants through Indian corporate establishment, technology transfer, and statutory regulatory compliance.

Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)

Navigating FEMA, RBI guidelines, and FIFP approvals for inbound equity investments, Wholly Owned Subsidiaries (WOS), and Joint Ventures.

Incorporation & ROC

Formation of Private & Public Limited companies and LLPs under the Companies Act 2013, drafting MOA/AOA, DIN/DSC, and annual statutory compliance.

Licensing & Franchising

Structuring cross-border technology transfers, royalty agreements, franchise frameworks, and non-disclosure covenants enforceable under Indian law.
