About GroverLaw

A boutique practice focused on substance over ceremony

We are an independent law practice based in New Delhi and Gurgaon. We advise Indian businesses, international enterprises, innovators, and founders on protecting their intellectual property and structuring cross-border business transactions.

Dual Legal Perspective

Vikram Grover qualified in India in 1996 and in England & Wales in 2002. This allows us to bridge common law principles with Indian statutory realities seamlessly, particularly in international contracts, cross-border technology transfers, and inbound investments.

In-House Search Databases

We maintain comprehensive in-house searchable records of published trademarks in the Trade Marks Journal, patents, and registered designs. This gives our clients fast, reliable clearance searches before they commit capital to brand launches or R&D.

Real Courtroom Experience

We appear before the High Courts across India (including the specialized Intellectual Property Division of the Delhi High Court), the Supreme Court of India, the Patent & Trademark Registries, and arbitration panels.

Our Focus

What we do for your business

We focus deeply on intellectual property, commercial contracts, and dispute resolution. Here is how we help clients every day:

Trademarks Domestic & Madrid Protocol

Brand Protection

Your brand is your reputation. We help you choose names and logos you can legally defend, register them in India and internationally, handle oppositions, and stop competitors from passing off their goods as yours.

Patents PCT & National Phase

Patents & Inventions

From drafting specifications to navigating Indian Patent Office objections and Computer Related Invention (CRI) guidelines, we protect technological advances for startups, researchers, and global corporations.

Designs & Code Kolkata Design Wing

Industrial Designs & Copyright

We register aesthetic product shapes under the Locarno system and protect original software code, creative content, and packaging against unauthorized duplication.

Enforcement Courts & Customs

Anti-Counterfeiting & Raids

When someone knocks off your products, we take decisive steps: court injunctions, search-and-seizure operations with Court Commissioners, police actions, and border control recordals under Customs rules.

Corporate FEMA & Companies Act

Commercial Contracts & FDI

Clear, enforceable agreements for licensing, technology transfers, franchising, and distribution. We also guide foreign companies setting up Indian subsidiaries and complying with RBI and FEMA guidelines.

Litigation Courts & ADR

Dispute Resolution

We represent clients in trial and appellate courts, arbitration, and mandatory pre-institution mediation under the Commercial Courts Act, 2015. We also handle .IN domain name disputes under INDRP.
